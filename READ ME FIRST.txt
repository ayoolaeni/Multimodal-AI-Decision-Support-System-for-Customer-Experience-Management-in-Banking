MULTIMODAL CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE DECISION SUPPORT
How to run it on your laptop (no coding needed)
============================================================

WHAT YOU NEED (one time only)
- A Windows laptop with an internet connection.
- Docker Desktop installed and opened at least once:
  https://www.docker.com/products/docker-desktop/


STEP 1 - UNZIP
Right-click the zip file and choose "Extract All".
TIP: extract it to a SHORT location such as  C:\mariam-app
(very long folder names can cause errors on Windows).


STEP 2 - OPEN DOCKER DESKTOP
Open Docker Desktop from the Start menu and wait until it says it is
running. Leave it open.


STEP 3 - START THE APP
Double-click  START_APP.bat  in the extracted folder.

- The FIRST time, it downloads and prepares everything. This can take
  10 to 20 minutes. Please be patient and do not close the black window.
- Later starts take under a minute.
- When it is ready, your web browser opens by itself at
  http://localhost:8502


STEP 4 - USE THE APP
The app has two pages, in the LEFT SIDEBAR:
1. The main page: a ranked list of customers, most urgent first.
2. "Live Triage": press "Simulate 1 complaint" or "Simulate 5 at once",
   or type your own complaint. Watch the AI agent read it, check the
   customer's transactions, rate it (Critical / High / Medium / Low),
   and suggest an action. The officer then clicks Approve or Dismiss.

The app only RECOMMENDS. It never contacts a customer or changes an
account. All data in it is invented sample data, not real customers.


STEP 5 - STOP THE APP
Double-click  STOP_APP.bat  when you are done.
To use it again, repeat Step 2 and Step 3.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- "Docker is not installed" or "Docker Desktop is not running":
  install or open Docker Desktop, wait until it says running, then
  double-click START_APP.bat again.
- "port is already allocated": another program is using port 8502.
  Close it and try again.
- The page does not open: wait a few more minutes on the first run,
  then go to http://localhost:8502 in Chrome or Edge.
- Still stuck: send a photo or screenshot of the black window to the
  person who gave you this folder.
